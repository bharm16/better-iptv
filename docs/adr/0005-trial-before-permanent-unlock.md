# Full trial before a permanent app unlock

Offer a free download with seven days of full access starting at the first successful channel playback, then require an explicit one-time Google Play purchase for continued access, with no app-inserted advertising. This lets viewers test their own provider and TV before paying and avoids consuming the trial during installation or failed setup. The app manages the trial separately from the permanent purchase; provider access remains separate from app access.

## Consequences

The [monetization research](../../.scratch/product-discovery/research-monetization.md) documents that a free-download listing cannot later become a paid-download listing under the same package, though in-app purchases remain available. Price, trial eligibility/reset behavior, timekeeping, expiry/offline policy, and purchase verification/restoration still need definition. No billing product or payment flow has been created.
