# Channel access is independent of guide data

The user reports unreliable or missing program listings from the providers the app must serve, so channel identity and playback are the foundation of the viewing experience. Home centers on favorite and recent channels, and guide data enriches Home, the guide, and channel browsing without being required to find or play a channel. This gives up a program-led discovery experience as the default in exchange for useful navigation when schedule information is sparse or unavailable.

The Guide therefore defaults to a channel list when schedule data is sparse, with a schedule grid available when listings are useful.
