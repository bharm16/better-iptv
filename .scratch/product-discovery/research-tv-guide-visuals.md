## Implication for the redraw

The observed guides organize the screen around aligned channel identities and program information, with a strong focus state and dedicated navigation/filter areas. Our earlier generic list/card recommendations did not demonstrate those proportions or structures. Use the full Sling image for composition and the crops for focus/filter detail; preserve Better IPTV’s channel-first sparse-data contract when deciding what replaces absent schedule information. This is a design inference, not a recommendation to fabricate listings or copy a competitor’s playback/session behavior.
