# IPTV Viewing

The language used to describe finding and watching content in the app.

## Language

**Viewer profile**:
A collection of favorites, viewing history, and preferences used to personalize the viewing experience. A viewer profile is distinct from a provider connection.

**App purchase**:
A one-time purchase of access to the player app, separate from access to a provider's television service.

**Trial**:
A seven-day period of full app access beginning with the first successful playback, used to evaluate the app with the viewer's provider and TV.

**App unlock**:
Continued app access granted by a one-time app purchase after or during the trial.

**Provider**:
An external television service from which the viewer accesses channels and related content.

**Provider connection**:
The viewer's saved access to a provider, identified by its server address and login credentials.

**Xtream Codes connection**:
A provider connection established using the provider's Xtream Codes interface and a server address, username, and password.

**Phone setup**:
A provider-connection setup flow in which the viewer scans a QR code on the TV and enters the connection details on a phone for that TV.

**Setup session**:
A temporary phone setup attempt linked to a specific TV.

**Live TV**:
Scheduled channel programming watched as it is being broadcast.

**Live window**:
The portion of an ongoing broadcast currently available for seeking or replay.

**Provider catch-up**:
Playback of earlier broadcasts from an archive supplied by the provider.

**Channel**:
A named stream of scheduled programming available through a particular provider connection.

**Channel lineup**:
The collection of channels available through a provider connection.

**Channel group**:
A provider-defined category of channels within a channel lineup.

**Program**:
Content scheduled to air on a channel during a particular time period.

**Guide data**:
Schedule information describing programs on channels, including their titles and broadcast times when supplied.

**Guide**:
The main view for browsing the selected provider's channel lineup, including schedule information when available.

**Schedule grid**:
A time-based presentation of channels and their scheduled programs within the Guide.

**Channel browser**:
A compact view for exploring recent channels and favorites while the current channel continues playing, with program information when available.

**Mini player**:
The compact playback view shown while the viewer browses Home or the Guide.

**Favorite channel**:
A channel the viewer explicitly saves for convenient access.

**Recent channel**:
A channel the viewer has watched recently.

**Home**:
The viewer's dedicated personal starting view, surfacing familiar content such as favorite and recent channels.
